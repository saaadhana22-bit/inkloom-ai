# Inkloom AI

### Your brand. Your voice. Every word.

Inkloom AI is a brand-focused content enhancement platform designed to help users create clearer and more consistent brand communication.

## The Problem

Brands often struggle to maintain a consistent communication style across different platforms and audiences. Generic content may fail to reflect a brand's identity and personality.

## Our Solution

Inkloom AI helps users refine their content according to their brand identity, communication tone, target audience and publishing platform.

## Key Features

- Brand personality selection
- Customisable communication tone
- Target audience and industry context
- Preferred and avoided words
- Platform-specific content guidance
- Content rewriting and enhancement
- Content quality indicators

## How It Works

1. Enter your brand details.
2. Select your brand personality and communication tone.
3. Add your target audience and publishing platform.
4. Paste your original content.
5. Analyse and rewrite your content.
6. Review the enhanced output.

## Technology Stack

- Python
- Streamlit

## Current Implementation

Inkloom AI currently uses a rule-based Python content enhancement engine. Further improvements and more advanced AI integration are potential future developments.

## Future Scope

- Advanced AI-powered content generation
- More personalised brand recommendations
- Expanded platform support
- Improved content analysis
